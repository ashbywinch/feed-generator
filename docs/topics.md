# SignalFlow — Curated Topic Set

13 topics. Sources are subscribed feeds that inform discovery for a topic (a feed may inform several); they are NOT a partition.

## 1. Grid & Net Zero economics

Energy generation, storage, transmission and grid balancing, and the economics and policy of the net-zero transition.

- **In:** Grid-scale batteries, solar/wind/pumped-hydro projects, CfD auctions, energy markets and pricing, grid data analytics (Modo Energy-style), climate-economics data.
- **Out:** Consumer EVs, energy-efficiency gadgets, corporate sustainability PR, climate activism.
- **Sources:** solarpowerportal, By the Numbers, Theory of Change, Carbon Brief

## 2. Data Centers / AI / macro

The AI industry layer: compute build-out, data centers, chips and silicon, model economics and AI capital markets.

- **In:** Data center construction, chip supply and geopolitics, AI market structure, funding and capex cycles, power constraints on compute.
- **Out:** AI tools for development/business (own topic), model benchmarks and product reviews, AI research papers.
- **Sources:** Elad Blog, Chamath Palihapitiya

## 3. Applied Life Sciences & Health

Therapeutic platforms, biotech scaling, and the etiology of complex chronic illness. Particular interest: ME/CFS and long COVID, multiple sclerosis and Alzheimer's, and new genetic, AI and longevity-related discoveries — but not at the expense of other health-tech innovation.

- **In:** Drug development and clinical research, biotech platforms and scaling, novel therapeutics, ME/CFS and long COVID research, multiple sclerosis and Alzheimer's, genetic/AI/longevity discoveries.
- **Out:** Wellness content, NHS operational politics, hospital anecdotes.
- **Sources:** Elad Blog

## 4. Geopolitics & Military Strategy

Doctrine, defense industrial capacity, great-power competition, and strategic/military analysis.

- **In:** Force structure and doctrine shifts, unmanned/autonomous systems, industrial base capacity, strategic analysis including military history.
- **Out:** Partisan politics, current-events punditry without strategic analysis.
- **Sources:** Palladium, Comment is Freed, A Collection of Unmitigated Pedantry

## 5. History (systems-thinking standpoint)

History analyzed through systems: logistics, incentives, physical constraints and technology adoption. Particular interest: how geography affects history, and the history of politics and economics.

- **In:** Innovation history (Age of Invention), quantitative/logistical history, how systems and constraints shaped outcomes, geography's role in history, history of politics and economics.
- **Out:** Narrative or political history without systems analysis, local nostalgia pieces.
- **Sources:** Age of Invention, by Anton Howes, Living London History, Spitalfields Life

## 6. Agriculture

Farming and food production as systems: net-zero agriculture, agronomy research, and the systems history of agriculture.

- **In:** Land use and agronomy research, agricultural policy, historical food systems, farm-environment interaction.
- **Out:** Home cooking, restaurant reviews, food science for chefs.
- **Sources:** The Allerton Project

## 7. Infrastructure & Policy (built environment)

Physical infrastructure and the built environment: construction costs, engineering structures, buildings and civil works, and their policy.

- **In:** Construction economics (Construction Physics), project delivery, engineering structures, urban form, infrastructure policy.
- **Out:** Transport operations and transit (own topic), energy generation (own topic).
- **Sources:** Construction Physics, Bad British Architecture, Notes on Growth

## 8. Transit

Public transit, roads and self-driving vehicles, and transport policy.

- **In:** Rail/bus/freight operations and investment, AV regulation and rollout, congestion pricing, transport innovation (London Reconnections-style).
- **Out:** Vehicle tech reviews, driving culture, EV consumer content.
- **Sources:** London Reconnections, Self Driving Insights, The Freewheeling Podcast

## 9. Industry, Manufacturing & Policy

Industrial production and manufacturing capability, supply chains, and industrial policy.

- **In:** High-mix manufacturing, reshoring and industrial base, energy-intensive industry, SMRs and heavy industry, defense industrial capacity (overlaps Geopolitics).
- **Out:** Consumer products, software, services.
- **Sources:** TOOL or DIE, HydraRaptor

## 10. Business, Risk & Finance

Markets, corporate strategy, risk and macro-finance.

- **In:** Capital allocation, market structure, economic analysis (Freakonomics-style), strategy (Rumelt), macro-finance.
- **Out:** Personal finance advice, stock tips, career advice.
- **Sources:** Freakonomics, Chamath Palihapitiya, The Rumelt Perspectives

## 11. Leadership / Management

Leading teams and organizations: management practice, organizational design, and product/engineering leadership.

- **In:** Coaching and management practice, org design, scaling engineering and product teams, leadership thinking.
- **Out:** HR compliance, generic career-advice columns.
- **Sources:** The Coaching Inn, Oren Ellenbogen's Thoughts, stories and ideas, Dark Matter and Trojan Horses, Sudo Make Me a CTO, Several People Are Coding, A Smart Bear, Community Building, Ravi Mehta | Product Leadership, QAspire Consulting – by Tanmay Vora

## 12. Innovation in Government

Public-sector digital transformation: government service design, platforms, and policy innovation.

- **In:** Government as a Platform, Policy Lab, user research in government, DWP/HMRC digital teams, gov design systems.
- **Out:** Party politics, general public-services commentary.
- **Sources:** Government as a Platform, Policy Lab, User research in government, Design in government, DWP Digital, Home Office Digital, Life at HMRC

## 13. AI Tooling for Development & Business

AI tools applied to software development and business workflows — the application layer, not the industry. Particular interest: open-source tools and open-weights models, plus frontier developments.

- **In:** AI coding assistants, AI agents for business, tool landscape and practical adoption, open-source tools and open-weights models, frontier AI tooling.
- **Out:** AI industry economics and capex (own topic), model research, benchmarks.
- **Sources:** Geoffrey Huntley, Signals in 5 Minutes ⏱️
