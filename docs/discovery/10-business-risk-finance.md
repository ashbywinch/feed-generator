### 1. Capital Allocation & Strategy

- **ACG Insights / Middle Market Growth** (`middlemarketgrowth.org`) — trade press — high · crawl: https://middlemarketgrowth.org/feed
  Middle-market M&A, private-equity deal flow, and buy-side investment analysis — distinct dedicated dealmaking source replacing the capitalallocators.com duplicate in Corporate Finance & Dealmaking while Capital Allocators stays in Capital Allocation & Strategy.
