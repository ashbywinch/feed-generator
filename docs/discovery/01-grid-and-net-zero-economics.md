# Grid & Net Zero economics — Discovery Source List

Status: **approved** by reviewer loop (iteration 2). 19 subareas, 64 sources, 5 queries. Generated 2026-08-01.

## Subareas
### 1. Grid-scale batteries & BESS economics

BESS revenue stacks (merchant, ancillary, capacity), cannibalisation, duration trends, co-location, project finance and the GB/EU pipeline.

- **Energy-Storage.news** (`energystorage.news`) — trade press — high
  Global trade press dedicated to grid-scale storage: project financings, deployment numbers, technology and market stories.
- **Modo Energy** (`modoenergy.com`) — data/analytics — high
  The reference GB grid-battery analytics house (the 'Modo Energy-style' source): asset-level revenue, ancillary price data and the 'Transmission' podcast on BESS economics.
- **Aurora Energy Research** (`auroraer.com`) — research/analytics — high
  Independent energy analytics firm publishing BESS revenue/cannibalisation forecasts and storage investment outlooks across GB/EU.

## News vs analysis
Analysis-leaning mix. Raw empirical signals come from registries/APIs (BMRS, ENTSO-E, LCCC, LBNL, NESO) and data houses (Modo Energy, EnAppSys, Energy-Charts, Ember); first-principles analysis from independent blogs and think tanks (Timera Energy, Watt-Logic, OIES, RAP, Grid Strategies) and journals (Energy Economics, Nature Energy); high-density news from trade press (Montel, Current News, reNEWS, Carbon Pulse, PV Magazine, Hydrogen Insight) and newsletters (Grid Brief, Volts); podcasts (Modo 'Transmission', Energy Gang, Redefining Energy, Energy Unplugged, Cleaning Up, Volts) add narrative context and expert interviews.
